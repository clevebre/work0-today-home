Help now

If you are in danger now, call 999. If you cannot speak, stay on the line; on a mobile, press 55 when asked, to let the police know it is an emergency.

Domestic and sexual abuse

Domestic and Sexual Abuse Helpline
0808 802 141424 hours a day, 365 days a year, for men and women affected by domestic or sexual violence, and for anyone worried about a friend or relative. Free from a landline or mobile; the number does not appear on a landline bill. Or email info@dsahelpline.org.
